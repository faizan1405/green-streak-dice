How to play
Tu aur ek Rival — dono ek saath ek real dice rolte ho, toh rolls hamesha barabar rehte hain.

Har player ko ek hidden, random count milta hai — kitne sides ko green karna hai (kisi ko nahi pata kiska zyada). Shuru mein dice ke 6 mein se sirf 1 face green-counting hai.

Yahi asli twist hai: jab green aata hai, dice ka ek aur face permanently green ban jaata hai — toh agla green aana aur aasaan. Jiske paas zyada green, uska agla roll utna zyada strong. Red aaye toh kuch nahi badalta.

Jo pehle saare sides green kar le, wahi jeeta. Bas rolte raho. 🟢
